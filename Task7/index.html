Transit Payout Checkout Redesign

Role: Lead Product Designer • Mobile Web & iOS • 6 weeks

Problem:Commuters abandon pass renewals at step four due to hidden fee labels and unclear card validation errors.

Approach & Outcome:Consolidated five screens into three focused steps with upfront fare summaries, cutting checkout drop-off by 31%.
